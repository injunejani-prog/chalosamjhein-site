How this calculator decides the months

  • Starts at 3 months of essential expenses, the low end of the common 3–6 month guideline.
  • Adds 1 month each if you're the only earner or have big EMIs.
  • Adds 2 months if your income is irregular.
  • Caps at 9 months. These steps are a simple illustration of the guideline, not a formula from a regulator.
  • The slider lets you pick any number of months from 3 to 9 yourself. Your choice is used for the target.
  • Dependents are already covered: their costs (school fees, parents' medical support) are part of your monthly expenses, so they are counted for every month of the fund. No extra month is added for them, so nothing is counted twice.
